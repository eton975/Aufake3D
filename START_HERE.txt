WHERE ARE THE DLLS AND RELEASE ZIP FILES?
  AFTER a successful build, go to the repository's dist/ folder:
    dist/core/a3d.dll
    dist/core/a3dapi.dll
    dist/Aufake3D_v0.9.2_Local_Runtime.zip
    dist/Aufake3D_v0.9.2_Descent3_Experimental_Win32.zip

GitHub's default SOURCE-CODE ZIP is NOT a compiled binary release. Download
verified release assets instead, or build the project with BUILD_WINDOWS.cmd
(Windows) / ./BUILD_LINUX.sh (Debian/Ubuntu).

Back up your games; start with low speaker volume. IMPORTANT: Alice (2011)
requires preservation of TWO genuine Miles DLL copies BEFORE installing its
proxy. Read Docs/INSTALLATION.md. Do not replace a whole SndDrivers folder.

Experimental Descent 3: use the separate public ZIP's COPY_TO_DESCENT3_GAME_FOLDER,
placing the two DLLs beside original main.exe and selecting Aureal 3D in
Descent 3 setup. Developer-only dist/descent3/ is NOT the public game patch.
